Privacy at RIP

Last updated: 9 November 2026

RIP RESALE LLC (“RIP”, “we”) runs resale.rip and logi.rip and sells used clothing, mostly on eBay. This page says what we collect, why we collect it, who handles it for us, and how to reach us.

The short version

  • On our websites, the only thing we ask for is your email address, and only if you sign up for updates.
  • Our websites use no cookies, ads or tracking. A simple counter tallies visits without cookies and without storing your IP address.
  • When you buy from us on eBay, eBay handles your payment and your address. We don’t keep your name, address or eBay username in our own records.
  • We never sell your information.

If you sign up for updates

  • What we collect: your email address, and when you signed up.
  • Why: to email you news about RIP: new drops, restocks and when the shop opens.
  • Who handles it: our email service, Kit, keeps the list and sends the emails. See Kit’s privacy policy.
  • Your choice: every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted completely, email us at privacy@resale.rip.
  • How long we keep it: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

When you visit our websites

  • We use no cookies, ads or tracking pixels.
  • We count visits with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and doesn’t store IP addresses. For each visit it keeps the page, the site you came from, your browser and operating system, your country, your language and your screen width. See GoatCounter’s privacy policy.
  • Our pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs every visitor’s IP address for security purposes.
  • Our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers to fetch them.
  • The light or dark setting you pick (and, on logi.rip, sound on or off) is saved in your own browser. It is never sent to us.

When you buy from us

  • We sell mainly on eBay. Your payment, your shipping address and your messages go through eBay, and eBay’s privacy policy covers them.
  • What we keep: the item, the order number, the price, eBay’s fees, the ship-by date and the tracking number. We need them to ship your order, keep our books and pay our taxes.
  • What we don’t keep: your name, address, phone number or eBay username. Shipping labels are bought and printed through eBay, and our packing lists never show a buyer’s name or address.
  • If eBay tells us an eBay member has closed their account, we delete anything we hold about them. Since we don’t keep buyers’ details, there is usually nothing to delete.

Where it’s kept and who sees it

  • Our records are kept in Microsoft’s cloud, in the United States, and on our own computers. Only the people who run RIP can see them.
  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t share it for advertising.
  • We disclose information only when the law requires it.

Children

Our websites and emails aren’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

When we change this page, we update the date at the top. If a change matters, we’ll say so in our next email.
